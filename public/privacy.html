Privacy at MNKISWAN Meadow

MNKISWAN Meadow is a free, cosy farm game by MNKISWAN Studio. The browser edition runs entirely on your own device: your farm never leaves your browser.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short

  • No account, no e-mail, no tracking cookies, no analytics, no ads.
  • Your farm, your farmer's name and your settings are saved only in this browser (IndexedDB and local storage).
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your farm for good.

Hosting

The game's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of each request (such as your IP address) to run and protect its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs.

Who makes it

MNKISWAN Meadow is made by MNKISWAN Studio, a two-person studio in India and Poland. Questions are welcome through mnkiswan.com/contact.

The game is built on the open-source project Harvest Hollow by Krasimir Kralev (MIT licence).
